# ADR-0008: v1.0 is M1 to M4; later milestones are demand-driven

* **Status:** Accepted (2026-11-15, by Dev1 under discretion delegated by the project owner; the owner may revise)
* **Blocks:** Roadmap

## Context

Ten validation apps on top of three products is more than a first release needs.

## Decision

v1.0 is released at the M4 exit: Core, three drivers, isolation, migrations and minimal Office. M5 onward is post-1.0 and each milestone starts when its validation app is wanted. Each milestone also has a time budget; when it runs out, scope is cut, not the gate.

## Consequences

* A usable, secure release arrives earlier.
* Events, compute, AI and the ledger are not promised on a date.
* Phase gates in TASKS.md still apply in order.
